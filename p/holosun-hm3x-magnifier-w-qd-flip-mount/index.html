HM3X Holosun HM3X Magnifier W/ QD Flip Mount The HM3X magnifiers will allow the operator to seamlessly transition from unmagnified to 3X magnification. The new Holosun HM3X features an adjustable diopter to dial in the desired focus, extremely clear lens and very generous eye relief.
